Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Last Reviewed: October 16, 2026
This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data by Lady Pilot ("Lady Pilot", "we", "us", "our") across the Lady Pilot Passenger mobile app, Lady Pilot Captain mobile app, and related web services.

🔒 Play Console Data Safety Commitment
Lady Pilot does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user personal data, telemetry, or contact records to third-party advertising or marketing brokers. All data collected is strictly necessary to perform core transit, passenger-captain safety matching, and emergency assistance.
CLAUSE 1

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Identification Data: When registering as a passenger or captain, we collect your Full Name, Email Address, Verified Mobile Number (via OTP verification), and Profile Photo.

B. Captain Compliance & KYC Data: To guarantee passenger safety, women drivers registering as Captains must submit Government Identity proof (Aadhaar Number and image), Permanent Driving License (DL), Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC), and Live Biometric Selfie for in-person administrative approval.

C. Real-Time Location Telemetry:

  • Foreground Location: We collect precise GPS latitude and longitude coordinates while the app is active on your screen to identify your pickup address, suggest nearby female captains, display route estimates, and navigate to the destination.
  • Background Location (Captains): The Lady Pilot Captain App collects background location data when the Captain sets their status to "Online / On Duty" or during an active assigned trip, even when the app is closed or minimized. This is strictly required to match nearby booking requests, provide turn-by-turn navigation, display live ETA to the waiting passenger, and detect safety route deviations. Background tracking immediately terminates the moment the Captain toggles "Off Duty" or logs out.

D. Device & Notification Telemetry: Device model, OS version, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens for ride alerts, IP address, and anonymized diagnostic crash logs.

CLAUSE 2

2. Purpose & Legal Basis of Processing

  • Core Ride Services: Connecting female passengers with verified female drivers, calculating upfront GPS fares, calculating shortest safe routes.
  • Safety & Emergency SOS: Monitoring active trips for unexpected route halts, telemetry sharing with designated emergency contacts, and immediate PCR police dispatch upon SOS button activation.
  • VoIP Number Masking: Calls between passengers and captains are routed through secure masked virtual relays. Captains and passengers never see each other's actual private telephone numbers.
  • Fraud Prevention & KYC: Verifying driving eligibility and platform integrity.
CLAUSE 3

3. Third-Party Infrastructure & SDK Disclosures

We work with trusted enterprise infrastructure providers to deliver our service:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android framework utilities and location provider services.
  • Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication, Cloud Messaging (FCM) for instant push notifications, and Firebase Realtime Database for live telemetry.
  • Mapbox / OpenStreetMap: Map tiles, route rendering, and geocoding.
  • Google Maps Navigation: Launched externally or via Picture-in-Picture for turn-by-turn driving guidance.

None of these vendors are granted rights to utilize your personal data for independent marketing purposes.

CLAUSE 4

4. Data Retention, Security & Deletion

All data transmitted between mobile devices and Lady Pilot cloud servers is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 encryption. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 standard.

Users have the unconditional right to request permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal records at any time directly through the in-app settings or via our dedicated web portal below.

Payouts are calculated weekly and remitted directly to the verified bank account or UPI ID on file.

4. Code of Conduct & Zero Tolerance Policy

We maintain zero tolerance for any verbal abuse, physical misconduct, discrimination, intoxication, or unauthorized ride solicitation outside the app. Violations will result in immediate police referral and platform ban.

5. Cancellation Policy

Passengers may cancel bookings free of charge within 3 minutes of captain assignment. Repeated frequent cancellations without justification after a captain has traveled toward pickup may incur a nominal cancellation fee credited directly to the dispatched driver for fuel compensation.
